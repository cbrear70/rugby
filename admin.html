Rugby Team Picker

Admin

Sign in

Use the admin account you created in Firebase (Authentication → Users). Coaches can't use this page.

Support requests

Sent from the app by club admins, and by team admins of teams that aren't in a club. Your answer shows on their phone under Contact support → Your requests; Email opens a reply to the address they gave. Safeguarding requests are listed first.

New club

A club groups its teams: club admins (invited here) can make teams, invite each team's admin, see every team's fixtures and make one parent code for the whole club. Put existing teams into a club with Add a team; their coaches, players and parents carry on as before.

New team

New teams join by invite: after creating one, tap Invite team admin on its card and send the link to whoever runs the team. They invite the coaches and hand out the player and parent codes from the app.

Development switches

Off hides the Pitch tab (selections and drills still work). Fixtures off hides the Fixtures tab, fixture choices on the Selection tab and the club calendar; nothing already entered is lost. Phones pick up a change when they next open or sync.

Teams

Codes are 10 letters or digits; capitals, spaces and dashes are sorted out for you. Random codes leave out I, O, 0 and 1 so they're easy to read out. Changing a join code copies the team's teams and moves to the new code and deletes the old one; coaches are asked for the new code on their next sync and keep everything. Changing a send code moves any waiting moves across; phones pick up the new send code on their next sync.
